About the App

TeaRush

Milk tea, made yours

TeaRush is a mobile ordering app built around one milk tea line, designed to make choosing, customizing, and getting your favorite drink as quick as possible. Browse the full menu, adjust sweetness and toppings to your taste, save your go-to orders, and grab exclusive promos — all without leaving the app.

Our objective

To give milk tea drinkers a simple, reliable way to order exactly what they want, while giving the shop a smoother, more organized way to receive and fulfill orders.
